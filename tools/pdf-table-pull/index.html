pdf-table-pull

Copying tables out of a PDF by hand is slow, and pasting them into Excel often wrecks the columns. pdf-table-pull does it in one step.

People with bank statements, invoices, supplier price lists, reports, or government data.

Honest limits

Text PDFs (where you can select the words with your mouse) give the best results. OCR on scans is slower and can misread blurry pages. Handwriting and photos of crumpled receipts will not work well.
