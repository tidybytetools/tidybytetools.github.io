json-shaper

Free converters handle neat CSV. json-shaper is for the messy stuff they choke on: notes, pasted tables, and — in the paid version — logs and addresses. Numbers stay numbers, and true/false become booleans.

People turning messy notes or exports into JSON for scripts and AI tools.

Honest limits

The address parser in the paid tool handles US one-line addresses only. If you only need plain CSV to JSON, the free lite tool is enough.
