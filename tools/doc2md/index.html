doc2md

AI tools read Markdown best. Most converters give you a wall of text, and tables turn into word soup. doc2md keeps headings, lists, and tables intact.

People feeding docs to ChatGPT, Claude, or their own AI app, building a search index, or moving old Word docs into a wiki.

Honest limits

The free lite tool reads text PDFs and saved HTML, not Word files and not scans. Two-column PDFs, like science papers, may mix the columns. Old .doc files and images are not read.
